WORD SENSE DISAMBIGUATION - ANALYSIS REPORT
=============================================

1. Objective
Identify the senses of ambiguous words using WordNet and apply
Lesk-style algorithms to resolve them in context.

2. Sense inventory
Target words: bank, bat, plant, book, mouse
Total senses: 59 (average 11.8 per word).
See output/sense_inventory.csv for glosses, synonyms, hypernyms,
hyponyms, antonyms and meronyms.

3. Methods compared
- First-sense baseline: most frequent WordNet sense.
- Simple Lesk: overlap between sentence context and sense gloss + examples.
- Extended Lesk: also uses hypernym, hyponym, meronym glosses and synonyms.

4. Results (accuracy on sample sentences)
method
extended_lesk    90.0
first_sense      50.0
simple_lesk      70.0

5. Error analysis (Extended Lesk misclassified):
- 'She read a book about ancient history.' -> bible.n.01: the sacred writings of the Christian religions

6. Observations
- Gloss overlap fails when the sentence shares few words with any gloss.
- Extending signatures with related synsets raises overlap coverage.
- Short sentences give little context, so ties fall back to the first sense.
- Future work: supervised WSD with scikit-learn using context features.
