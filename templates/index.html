Word Sense Disambiguation

WordNet sense inventory, lexical relations, Lesk and supervised WSD

1. Sense Inventory and Lexical Relations

2. WSD on Sample Text

4. Evaluation and Analysis

Accuracy of each method on the sample sentences.
